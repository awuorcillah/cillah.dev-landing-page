**Turning Social Media Attention Into Qualified Sales Opportunities**

* Real estate companies are generating more property enquiries through Instagram, Facebook, websites, TikTok, and advertising campaigns than ever before.

But generating the enquiry is only the beginning.

A prospect may comment on a property post, send a direct message, submit a website enquiry, or respond to an advertisement. From there, someone needs to manually respond, collect the right information, understand what the prospect is looking for, determine whether they are genuinely interested, and make sure the enquiry reaches the right sales agent.

When this process is handled manually, valuable enquiries can be delayed, missed, or lost between teams and different customer-facing channels.

***The Problem With Managing Enquiries Manually***

A typical property enquiry may require several steps:

* Responding to the prospect
* Collecting their contact details
* Understanding their property requirements
* Identifying their budget and preferred location
* Determining whether they are looking to buy or rent
* Qualifying the enquiry
* Assigning the lead to the appropriate agent
* Sharing the conversation context with the agent
* Recording the lead in the CRM
* Following up

As enquiry volume increases, keeping up with these repetitive tasks becomes increasingly difficult.

***AI automation can handle many of these first steps automatically.***

A prospect can interact with a property through a comment, direct message, website form, advertisement, or another digital channel.

The system can then:

1\. Capture the enquiry

2\. Respond immediately

3\. Collect relevant information

4\. Qualify the prospect

5\. Identify the type of property they are interested in

6\. Determine their location, budget, and requirements

7\. Assign the enquiry to the appropriate sales agent

8\. Send the agent the conversation context

9\. Record the lead in the CRM

The result is a structured process that moves the enquiry from initial interest to a sales-ready opportunity.

***What This Looks Like in Practice***

Imagine someone sees a property on Instagram and comments asking for more information.

Instead of waiting for a member of the sales team to notice the comment, the system can respond immediately and continue the conversation in their inbox.

The prospect can provide information such as:

* Name
* Phone number
* Email
* Buying or renting preference
* Preferred location
* Property type
* Budget
* Number of bedrooms
* Other requirements

The AI can then organise this information and determine whether the enquiry meets the company's qualification criteria.

The relevant sales agent receives the lead together with the conversation context, giving them everything they need to continue the conversation.

Your Sales Team Stays in Control

Automation does not have to replace the sales team.

Instead, it can take care of repetitive first-line tasks while sales agents focus on conversations that require human judgement with qualified leads.

Agents can receive structured, qualified enquiries instead of having to manually work through every incoming message.

This means the sales team can spend more time speaking with serious prospects and less time performing repetitive administrative work.

24/7 Enquiry Handling

***People do not only enquire during business hours.***

A prospect may discover a property late at night, during a weekend, or while the sales team is unavailable.

An automated system can continue handling the initial interaction, collecting information, and preparing the enquiry for the sales team.

When the agent starts their day, the relevant information is already organised and ready for follow-up.

From Enquiry to Sales Opportunity

The goal is not simply to automate replies.

The goal is to create a reliable process that moves people from:

Property Interest → Enquiry → Qualification → Agent Assignment → Follow-Up → Sales Opportunity

By connecting customer-facing channels with AI automation and the CRM, real estate companies can create a more consistent process for managing the enquiries already being generated by their marketing.

The Opportunity

Every comment, message, website enquiry, and advertisement response represents potential business.

The challenge is making sure those opportunities do not disappear because nobody responded quickly enough, collected the right information, or followed up.

With the right automation system, real estate companies can build a process that captures and qualifies these opportunities continuously, while keeping their sales team involved where human interaction matters most.
