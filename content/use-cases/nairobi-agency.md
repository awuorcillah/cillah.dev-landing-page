a nairobi realestate agency was consistently posting luxury townhouses, villas, and apartments across Instagram and Facebook. Their content was working. The problem was what happened after the engagement.

## The Problem

Every day, potential buyers were asking the same question:

“Can I get more information on this property?”

Those inquiries were coming from:

- Instagram comments
- Instagram DMs
- Facebook comments
- Facebook Messenger
- WhatsApp
- Paid ads
- TikTok
- Google campaigns

And all of that traffic had to pass through two administrators before reaching the sales team.

Imagine being responsible for replying to hundreds of property inquiries every day while also distributing leads fairly across multiple agents. Then imagine doing it again tomorrow. And the next day.

The internet never sleeps. Buyers were inquiring at midnight, early morning, and throughout the day. Foreign investors were browsing Nairobi properties from different time zones.

The agency had a strong marketing engine, but its growth was being limited by a human bottleneck.

## What We Discovered

The sales team didn’t need more traffic.

They needed a system that could:

- Respond instantly
- Qualify buyers automatically
- Route leads to the right agent
- Keep conversations moving after hours
- Give management visibility across all channels

## The Solution

We implemented a unified AI-driven inquiry management system.

The system connected:

- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- Website inquiries
- Lead routing workflows

Instead of relying on manual replies for every inquiry, AI agents handled the initial conversation, answered common questions, collected buyer details, and passed qualified leads directly to the sales team.

Management could also monitor every conversation from a single inbox and watch AI agents interact with customers in real time.

## The Outcome

- **Faster** response times
- **Less** manual workload
- **More** qualified viewings

The biggest change wasn’t just automation.

It was operational capacity.

The agency moved from a workflow that depended on constant human availability to a system that could handle high-volume digital traffic continuously.

Instead of reacting to inquiries, the team could focus on closing deals.

## The Real Insight

Social media has made real estate attention global.

The agencies that win are not necessarily the ones posting the most content.

They are the ones that can capture, qualify, and follow up on every inquiry before a competitor does.

That realization became the foundation of Cillah.ai.
